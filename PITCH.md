# Moodbuilder — the flow, as a pitch

For a design lead. The claim, the flow, and a breakdown of every step so we can
check that each one earns its place and ladders up to what the product promises.

> Forward-facing companion to `FLOW_AUDIT.md` (the working analysis behind it).
> Together these supersede the "North star" and "Journey" sections of NEXT.md /
> VISION.md, to be trimmed once the direction is signed off.

---

## 1. The claim — what Moodbuilder is

A **brand-direction tool whose wedge is synthesis from your *own* taste** — not
generation, not a stock-template gallery. It sits **upstream of Figma**: the layer that
resolves the fog *before* the execution decision, then hands off.

The promise, in the user's words (validated in dogfooding):

> "I don't need the machine to tell me my taste. I need it to give me tools to
> *extract* my taste from inspiration."

And the emotional job it's hired for:

> **overwhelm → relief → tangible.** Take someone drowning in disparate inspiration
> and gut feeling, and get them to something concrete enough to move on (and to
> *show* a team or client).

Three principles a design lead should hold us to:

- **Recognition, not articulation.** Users know it when they see it; the tool reads the
  YES (and the informative NO) and converges. It does not ask them to specify a brief forward.
- **You stay the author.** The tool hands you the brush (react, pull, arrange, tidy). It
  never decides your taste *for* you, and it never hides what it did.
- **The mess resolves to order.** The divergent half is tactile and intentionally messy;
  it snaps to clean structure as you converge. The emotional arc, made physical.

## 2. The shape, in one line

> **Inspiration → Direction → Brand.**   (overwhelm → relief → tangible)

Three nouns a stranger gets in one read. Four verbs over them: **gather, organize,
narrow, compose.** That is the entire mental model.

## 4. Step-by-step breakdown

Each step: what arrives, the move the user makes, what it produces, the claim it proves,
and where it stands today.

### GATHER — react & pull
```
  IN     your imported inspiration (pins from Pinterest / Are.na)
  DO     react to each pin (yes / sure / maybe / meh / nope); the colors of what
         you keep collect; pick a pin's colors by eye; say the "why" in your words
  OUT    a pool of resonant references + colors + your first words
  PROVES recognition-not-articulation · you-stay-the-author · cultivate-don't-supply
  STATUS ✅ validated — the react loop genuinely narrows; "saw it organized for me"
```
*The tool supplies the instrument (fast react); the human supplies the taste. Type is
gathered here too. This is the one place color gets curated from pins.*

### ORGANIZE — the board (mess ⇄ tidy)
```
  IN     what you gathered
  DO     rifle the tactile PILE and pull cards onto your board; flip the board between
         MESS (free, hand-arranged collage) and TIDY (neat per-dimension lanes: color,
         image, type) — reversible, your arrangement never lost; annotate with comment pins
  OUT    one master board: your inspiration, made legible, in your hands
  PROVES overwhelm→relief made physical · you-stay-the-author · the-whole-over-the-parts
  STATUS 🔶 pile feeling validated (the spike); mess⇄tidy toggle is the next build
```
*This is the signature moment and the differentiator. The relief is reversible: order
on demand, mess one tap away. It absorbs three old concepts (auto-sort zones, manual
sections, the library inventory) into one gesture.*

### NARROW — pull a few directions
```
  IN     the master board (everything)
  DO     pull a few coherent DIRECTIONS out of the pile (the same pull gesture, onto
         new boards) — each a curated collage heading somewhere
  OUT    a few named directions, each a candidate identity
  PROVES the funnel (broad → a few directions) · you-stay-the-author
  STATUS 🔶 the act is validated as needed; the exact gesture is being simplified
```
*"A board is a direction." This is where overwhelm becomes a small number of tangible
bets you can compare and socialize.*

### COMPOSE — direction → brand
```
  IN     a direction (its colors, type, the "why," provenance)
  DO     watch it become a brand: two live previews (dark/light), shuffle the palette,
         click any element to recolor, set type, check legibility; compare and share
         for feedback — all aimed at the DirectionCard, the artifact that travels
  OUT    a composed brand identity tied back to its source
  PROVES connected-to-source · briefing-instrument (not designer-replacer) · tangible
  STATUS 🔶 previews + DirectionCard validated; the rail is being cut from 9 panels to the core
```
*The DirectionCard is the protagonist — present from Gather on, filling with your
material and words, and it is what you hand a collaborator or future-you.*

### DELIVER — make it real, hand it off
```
  IN     a composed brand
  DO     export the deliverable: a brand book (PDF), design tokens for Figma, a share
         link that keeps provenance
  OUT    something to show a team / client, or take into execution
  PROVES the pre-Figma seam (hand off tokens, not a flattened screenshot) · done-state
  STATUS 🔷 the brand-book exists; token export comes once Compose is stable
```

## 5. Why it holds together (the pitch to the lead)

- **One spine, three nouns.** Inspiration → Direction → Brand. No room hallway, no jargon
  to memorize. The four verbs are things you *do*, not objects.
- **The emotional arc is the architecture.** overwhelm (the pile) → relief (it tidies) →
  tangible (a brand you can show). The mess⇄tidy toggle makes that arc a gesture, which is
  the thing no execution tool (Figma, Canva) does, because they serve the decision *after*
  the fog, not the fog itself.
- **It defends the wedge.** Every step keeps the human as author and stays linked to source.
  We are a *briefing instrument* that gets you to the YES, not a generator that hands you a
  taste you didn't choose.
- **De-risked.** The load-bearing moments are already validated by real dogfooding (the
  react loop; the "organized for me" relief; the pile feeling). We are simplifying down to
  what's proven, not betting on the unproven.

## 6. What we cut to get here (so the simplicity is deliberate, not naive)

The current build had drift: two parallel navigation systems, ~6 names for "your
inspiration," color curated in 3 places, and a Compose screen with 9 stacked panels.
The new direction is a **subtraction**:

- One navigation (the four stages), retiring the old 7-step path.
- One word for your inspiration; the pile is its *view*, not a separate place.
- One place to curate color (Gather + the board).
- Compose cut to its core: the artifact, the two previews, shuffle, recolor, type, legibility.
- Shelved until validated: the cross-project "well," standalone gradients, marks, textures,
  the long export menu.

Full per-item reasoning and the keep/merge/cut/defer calls are in `FLOW_AUDIT.md`.
