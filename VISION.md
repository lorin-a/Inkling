# Moodbuilder — Product Vision & Build Plan

*Living source of truth for the recognition-driven, co-design brand studio. Pairs
with NEXT.md (session log / next moves) and the `project_product_direction` memory.
Last shaped 2026-06-02 with Lorin.*

---

## 1. What it is (the soul)

A tool that helps you **find your brand the way you actually find it: by recognizing
it, not describing it.** You can’t write a brief for a feeling you haven’t seen yet,
but you know it when you see it. The tool registers that recognition, draws your
intuition into the open, and turns it into something tangible you can act on and hand
to others.

It serves the **fog before the decision** — which is why it isn’t Figma or Canva.
Those execute a choice already made. This lives upstream, where you have a board full
of inspiration, a strong-but-vague gut feeling, and no words for it yet.

The origin and wedge: **synthesis from your actual taste, never generation.** It
starts at your real inspiration (your Pinterest board, your references) and distills
*that* into a direction. It is yours, surfaced — not a machine’s idea of pretty.

It is fundamentally a **co-creation tool.** Solo and collaborative are the *same
canvas*: a workshop of one that scales to a client or a team. The brand gets found
*together*.

---

## 2. The emotional core

**Overwhelm → relief → tangible.** Someone is drowning in disparate inspiration and
inchoate feeling. The tool turns that pile into a few things they can hold, gets them
unstuck, and surprises them along the way. Every surface earns that relief. A surface
that adds overwhelm, or makes a decision for the user, is failing the core job.

---

## 3. Principles & hard rules (inviolable)

1. **Recognition, not articulation.** The user reacts; the tool reads the yes *and*
   the informative no. It does not ask them to specify forward.
2. **Cultivate intuition, don’t supply it.** The tool helps the person find the words
   *within themselves* — it never hands them their vocabulary. It prompts:
   *“What do these have in common in your eyes? What do they evoke?”* and for a no,
   *“What isn’t working here?”* Mirror and midwife, not oracle. (This is also the
   co-design “I like / I wish / what if” move — same mechanism.)
3. **You stay the author.** Hand-curation is primary and tactile; shuffle/react is a
   strategic power-tool, not the spine.
4. **No silent narrowing.** Never drop, merge, cluster, cap, sample, or re-order the
   user’s material and present it as “all of it.” If something is summarised, the
   full set stays reachable and labelled. Transparency over tidiness.
5. **The no matters, in her words.** Rejection is informative; it’s captured as the
   user’s own language, and it becomes an explicit “avoid” in any brief.
6. **Synthesis from your taste, never generation.** Always grounded in the user’s own
   pool.
7. **One canvas is the heart.** The rooms (brand, colours, library) become tools
   summoned onto it. The well is always present; everything stays linked to source
   (credit preservation is a hard requirement).
8. **Collaboration is optional by default.** Timing optional (never box in slower
   movers); anonymity optional; roles optional — but a client/designer distinction
   must persist throughout when used.
9. **Don’t over-engineer.** Proportion the build to the problem. (e.g. “drop into
   context” = 5–10 curated free mockups, not a mockup maker.)

---

## 4. The mechanic: recognition + co-design facilitation

The interaction is grounded in **participatory-design / co-design workshop
facilitation**, not invented from scratch: pool → sort → affinity-cluster → vote →
synthesize; “I like / I wish / what if”; **silent independent reaction before the
group reveal** so no one anchors on the loudest voice. Playing and shuffling identity
*together* until it clicks. The same recognition primitive serves every user; only
the framing and scaffolding change.

## 5. Audience: five users

| | Proficiency | Purpose | Mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Maya** | None (non-designer founder) | Get unstuck, brief better | Solo → handoff |
| **Nadia** | Pro (researcher / facilitator) | Run co-creation *with* a team | Collaborative-primary |
| **Theo** | Pro (designer) | Personal exploration until it clicks | Solo |
| **Priya** | Non-designer stakeholder | React / vote on someone’s directions | Async contributor |
| **Sam** | Learning (student) | Learn the method, build a brand | Solo, scaffolded |

**Maya — the founder who can’t say it.** Hired a designer, got something competent
she doesn’t feel *yes* about, can’t explain why.
- *Needs:* zero jargon; gut reactions; her intuition treated as valid; **prompts that
  draw her own words out**; a hand-off brief that says “this, not that, and the feeling.”
- *Fears:* her taste is “wrong”; being railroaded; wasting money; the tool deciding
  for her.

**Nadia — the co-design facilitator** (closest to Lorin). Brings a founder + team to
converge *together*.
- *Needs:* the workshop arc as structure; multi-contributor input; **independent
  reaction before reveal**; rationale captured in participants’ words; the **tensions**
  surfaced, not just consensus; an exportable synthesis to present back. All timing /
  anonymity / roles **optional**.
- *Fears:* groupthink and premature convergence; the tool feeling like a toy next to
  her method; losing the “why”; one voice dominating.

**Theo — the pro on a passion project** (the Whelm case). Intuition-led, no client,
wants to play.
- *Needs:* fast tactile play; surprise and range; **lock what he loves, shuffle the
  uncertain**; freedom to leave and return to the well; **drop a direction onto a
  curated mockup** to feel it.
- *Fears:* over-deliberating and killing the feeling; getting stuck; the tool
  flattening his range.

**Priya — the invited stakeholder.** Time-poor, not a designer, on her phone.
- *Needs:* a 30-second, no-account react; permission to not justify; **react-then-
  reveal** (sees the group only after she submits); **voice react** for verbalizers;
  to feel her input landed.
- *Fears:* being asked *why*; decision fatigue; her vote vanishing.

**Sam — the learner.** Building a brand for class/portfolio, learning the method.
- *Needs:* scaffolding that mirrors a real method (named: affinity map, dot vote);
  the *why* shown; a repeatable arc; a **“show your work”** process export.
- *Fears:* blank-canvas paralysis; no “right” process; imposter feeling.

**Through-line:** every fear is about being *judged, railroaded, or unheard*. The
tool must protect each person’s authority over their own taste.

## 6. Flow maps

```
MAYA (solo → handoff)
 import board → react gut → prompt: "what do these share / evoke?" (her yes, her words)
   + on a no: "what's not working?" → a direction forms from what resonates
   → (optional) invite cofounder to react → export a brief IN HER WORDS (yes + no + feel)

NADIA (facilitator, collaborative)
 create session → invite (client/designer roles, optional) → each reacts independently
   (optional: anonymous, untimed) → REVEAL → consensus + tensions surfaced
   → cluster together → reflective prompts capture the "why" → vote → few directions
   → synthesize → present back / export

THEO (solo pro, play)
 dump pool + clipped resources → react to narrow → assemble a direction
   → lock what you love, shuffle the uncertain → drop onto a curated mockup to feel it
   → return to the well, try another → land a direction

PRIYA (async contributor)
 open link (no account) → react to a handful (tap or voice) → submit
   → THEN see where the group landed → (optional) notified her pick moved forward

SAM (learner)
 start from a guided/curated pool → react with light "why this matters" (named:
   affinity map, dot vote) → assemble a direction, reasoning visible
   → export "show your work" + the brand
```

**What the flows expose:** every persona touches the same five systems. Solo and
collaborative are the *same model* with contributors set to one or many.

## 7. Architecture: the five systems

1. **The well (sources).** Every source is one kind of thing — your pins, clipped
   external resources, the curated library. Ingestion: URL → image + metadata +
   palette + source (credit preserved). Includes an **inspiration inbox** (a holding
   pen before you sort) and a **curated resource library** by category (fonts/
   foundries, colour, texture, galleries, tools, reading). The “one place, not 50
   bookmarks” promise. Paste-URL first; browser clipper/extension later.
2. **The reaction system.** Per-person, **private-until-reveal**, carrying the
   reflective-prompt words as metadata (the yes language, the no language, optional
   confidence/emotion). Solo = one participant; collaborative = many. *This shape is
   built from day one so adding contributors is wiring, not a rewrite.*
3. **The direction.** The synthesis of what resonated — a moodboard (yeses +
   distilled colours/aspects + the user’s words). A board *is* a direction. Many
   directions per project. Feeds the Brand studio.
4. **Sessions / sharing.** Async first (builds on the existing
   `instances/sessions/votes` schema); live co-sort later (CRDT). Roles
   (client/designer), anonymity, timing — all optional.
5. **Export.** A brief in the user’s words (Maya), a facilitator synthesis with
   consensus + tensions + quotes (Nadia), a “show your work” process doc (Sam), the
   brand book (existing).

**Grounded generation** (where used — never to supply taste): structured output
always on the user’s own pool. e.g. proposing a *prompt*, never an answer.

## 8. Feature set, tiered (don’t overshoot)

**Tier 1 — the intuition spine (first)**
1. Recognition triage — react through your own pool. *(built)*
2. **Reflective prompts** — draw out the user’s own words for the yes and the no. *(new, the differentiator)*
3. Distill what resonates — colours now, aspects later; nothing hidden. *(built)*
4. The well + inspiration inbox + curated library — paste-URL ingestion first.
5. **The seam forward** — triage produces *a direction* that feeds Brand. *(stops the island)*

**Tier 2 — co-creation + play**
6. Async multi-contributor — invite; react-then-reveal; optional roles/anonymity/timing.
7. Divergence + consensus view — agreement *and* tensions.
8. Lock + shuffle the uncertain.
9. Voice react (verbalizers).

**Tier 3 — feel + learn**
10. Drop into 5–10 curated mockups.
11. Scaffolding / method-named guidance.
12. Recommendations from taste · “your input mattered” · “show your work” export.

**Frontier (parked):** live real-time co-sort · perceptual aspect-remix · version
branching · browser-extension clipper.

## 9. Build plan (phased — one spine, many lenses)

**We are not building for one persona.** All five share one substrate (Section 7);
they differ only in framing and scaffolding. So the personas are **lenses on one
spine**, not separate products. The two traps to avoid: building a narrow product for
one persona, and building abstract “platform-for-everyone” infra validated by no one.

The discipline that avoids both: **build the shared spine, but validate it through one
concrete, dogfoodable path end-to-end.** Then the build order is forced by the
*dependency graph*, not by persona preference:

- The spine is **solo at its core** (one person reacting + reflecting).
- **Collaboration** (Nadia, Priya) is multi-participant *added on top* — impossible
  without the solo reaction model first.
- **Per-persona leaves** (Maya’s brief, Sam’s scaffolding/why, the curated-resource
  lens) are configuration on shared data, added once the spine is proven.

So: **build the spine collab-ready and lens-ready from day one**, dogfood it on Whelm
(Lorin’s own case = highest-signal), and every persona afterward is *wiring on a
proven substrate*, not a rebuild. The rule still holds: **a thin connected vertical
end-to-end before deepening any node.**

- **Phase 0 — Connect the island (now, small).** Wire the existing triage → a
  *direction* (a moodboard from the yeses + gathered colours) → Brand, via the
  shipped Board→Brand seam. The gathered material must flow somewhere.
- **Phase 1 — Intuition spine, solo, end-to-end thin.** Reflective prompts (your
  words, yes + no) · the well + inspiration inbox + curated library (paste-URL) ·
  output a direction + a brief in your words. *Ship and dogfood Whelm through it whole.*
- **Phase 2 — Co-creation, async.** Sessions + invite + per-person reactions +
  reveal + consensus/tension. Optional roles/anonymity. Builds on the votes schema.
- **Phase 3 — Play + feel.** Lock-and-shuffle · curated mockups · voice react.
- **Phase 4 — Learn + frontier.** Scaffolding/why · recommendations · then live
  co-sort, aspect remix.

## 10. First slice (concrete — to prove the spine, not stall)

**Goal:** one thin, connected, dogfoodable vertical that is unmistakably *this*
product: react → reflect in your own words → a named direction → wired toward Brand.

1. **Reflective prompt** on the recognition surface. After the user has kept a few
   pins (or via an always-available “Reflect” affordance), show a prompt card on the
   gathered side: *“Looking at what you’ve kept — what do these have in common in your
   eyes? What do they evoke?”* with a free-text field. A parallel no-prompt is
   reachable from any pin marked Meh/Nope: *“What’s not working here?”* Her answers
   save as part of the direction. The tool never autofills them.
2. **Make a direction.** A “Make a moodboard from this” action creates a moodboard
   from the kept pins (image blocks, credit preserved) + the gathered colours (swatch
   blocks) + her words (a note block). Reuses the `moodboards` store.
3. **Wire to Brand.** From the direction, “Compose a brand” deep-links `/brand` with
   that board as the source (the Board→Brand seam already composes colours from a
   board). Nothing new in Brand.
4. **Persistence:** localStorage signed-out, DB authed — existing patterns.

Outcome: from a pile of Whelm pins to a named direction (with Lorin’s own words) that
opens in Brand. End-to-end, connected, in the vision.

## 11. Already built / reuse

- **Recognition triage** — `/recognize`: whole-field board of all pins, focused
  react (YES/Sure/Maybe/Meh/Nope), gathered colours (curated + All toggle), eyedropper
  to pick colours off the image, onboarding tour. `lib/recognition.js` engine.
- **Spatial canvas** — `/moodboard`: drag/drop image/swatch/type/shape blocks, crop,
  finishes. `moodboards` store (file / DB / localStorage).
- **Board → Brand seam** — `/brand` composes an identity from a board’s colours.
- **Brand / Colors / Gradients / Decide / Print / Library / Resources** rooms.
- **Deferred Moodvote schema** (`instances/sessions/votes`) — the home for async
  collaboration.
- **Grounded engines** — `composePalette`, `colorTheory`, `fontPairings`,
  palette extraction.
