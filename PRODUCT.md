## Users

Lorin first — a design researcher (MA Design, CMU) who thinks like a designer and builds
like a developer — using Inkling for her own projects and remote client collaborations.
Then, designers and design-literate creators who already have taste and saved inspiration.
NOT built down for non-designers. Context: someone overwhelmed by a pile of saved
references, wanting to converge on a brand direction they author themselves.

## Product Purpose

A **brand-direction studio** that turns your *actual saved inspiration* (a Pinterest/Are.na
board, screenshots, links) into a real brand direction — palette, type, a wordmark, a book,
exportable tokens — with **you as the author the whole way.** The wedge is *synthesis from
your taste*, not generation and not curation. It's the **pre-Figma starting point**: the
fog-resolving layer upstream of Figma; handoff is the distilled direction as tokens, never
the re-hosted inspiration board. One home (the canvas), three moments: **Gather · Play ·
Build.** Success = the overwhelm → relief → tangible arc actually lands, and the tool helps
you find your direction without deciding it for you.

## Brand Personality

**Editorial, instinctive, authoring.** Three words: *editorial, warm, exacting.* Voice is a
quiet, structured frame around the user's art — confident and craft-driven, never chirpy or
salesy. It supplies instruments; the human supplies the taste. Emotional goal: relief and
trust ("you know it when you see it"), the feeling of a credible design tool made by someone
who cares about typography, motion, and spatial hierarchy.

## Anti-references

- **NOT Canva, NOT a "cheesy Michaels scrapbook."** A credible design tool for creators;
  warmth comes from *craft* (paper, type, light, space), never literal props/stickers.
- **NOT generic AI dashboard UI** — no card-grid monotony, no hero-metric template, no emoji.
- **The chrome must NEVER distract from or recolour the user's art** — the pins are the
  hero/colour, shown true; the UI is a quiet editorial frame.
- **No motion that bounces, springs, or overshoots** (Apple-restraint easing only).
- **No silent narrowing** — never drop/merge/cluster/cap/re-order user data and present it
  as "all of it" (full set always one tap away, count visible).

## Design Principles

1. **The human stays the author.** Supply the brush, never the verdict. Anything that decides
   for her violates it; anything that hands her the instrument honours it.
2. **Recognition over articulation.** Users know it when they see it; read the YES *and the
   informative NO* and converge. A briefing instrument, not a replacement designer.
3. **Cultivate intuition, don't supply it.** Draw HER words out with reflective prompts; never
   hand her the vocabulary ("your taste = earthy/quiet").
4. **Simplicity at home, sophistication on demand.** The canvas stays calm and legible; depth
   lives inside the focused overlays you summon.
5. **Overwhelm → relief → tangible.** Every surface serves that arc; mess (divergent stages)
   resolves into clean order at Build.
6. **Sophistication = clean structure + intentional accents,** never round-and-playful. The
   art is the hero; warmth from craft, not props.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor: 44×44 touch targets, visible `:focus-visible`, `prefers-reduced-
motion` honoured per component (content stays visible when motion is off — reveals enhance an
already-visible default). Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on the warm-paper ground.
